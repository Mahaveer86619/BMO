Multimeter Test Guide

1. Continuity Test (Power OFF)

Set your multimeter to Continuity Mode (the icon that looks like a sound wave). With the Pico unplugged:

  • Touch one probe to OLED GND and the other to Pico Pin 3. It should beep.
  • Touch one probe to OLED VCC and the other to Pico Pin 36. It should beep.

Ensure no beep between VCC and GND (Short circuit check)!

2. Voltage Test (Power ON)

Plug in the Pico. Set multimeter to DC Voltage (20V):

ProbesExpected Voltage
Black on Pico Pin 3, Red on Pico Pin 36~3.3V
Black on Pico Pin 3, Red on OLED VCC~3.3V

3. I2C Logic High (Power ON)

The I2C lines (SDA/SCL) should be "Pulled High" to 3.3V when idle.

  • Red probe on OLED SDA, Black on GND: Should show ~3.3V.
  • Red probe on OLED SCL, Black on GND: Should show ~3.3V.
